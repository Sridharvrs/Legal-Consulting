Our Story

Three Decades of Dedicated Legal Service

What began as a two-attorney practice in a modest Manhattan office has flourished into a full-service legal consulting firm trusted by clients across the nation.

Our founder, Robert Stackly, established the firm on a simple principle: every client deserves the same caliber of legal representation regardless of the size or complexity of their case. That principle remains our guiding philosophy today.

Over the decades, we have expanded our practice areas, attracted some of the finest legal minds in the country, and built a reputation for achieving results through a combination of strategic thinking, meticulous preparation, and unwavering advocacy.
