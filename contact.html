Quick Answers

Is the initial consultation free?

Yes, your first 30-minute consultation is complimentary. We believe everyone deserves access to quality legal advice before making a commitment.

Bring any relevant documents, timelines, and a written summary of your situation. The more context you provide, the more productive our conversation will be.
